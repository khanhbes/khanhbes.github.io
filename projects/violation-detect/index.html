01 The challenge

From detecting vehicles to understanding violations.

Vietnamese roads combine dense, mixed traffic with complex lane behavior. A useful system must do more than draw boxes: it needs to preserve identity across frames, interpret road context and produce evidence that people can review.

This research builds a closed-loop platform around that challenge—from camera input and AI inference to violation records, notifications, payment and appeals.

05 Evaluation

Balanced accuracy and real-time performance.

After 150 training epochs, the segmentation model reached strong detection and mask quality while preserving the throughput needed for live monitoring.

Benchmark reported on NVIDIA A100. The 7.2 ms figure measures inference only; complete system latency also depends on decoding, tracking, networking and storage.
